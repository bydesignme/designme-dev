Clean, batch 17

OTP field, Form, Payment form, Number field and Carousel, each on its own page in Clean, with the batch 1 and 2 notes and your latest notes met in every style. Switch the style and light or dark in the header, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • OTP fieldIts content sits 16px in from every edge in every style (Clean's was 7px at the top and 0 at the sides). The line under the boxes and the resend line fold open and shut, so the card keeps no blank line for them and eases to its new height. Checking the code uses the moving highlight. The right code is the green tick and plain words, with the boxes edged in green and no green fill; the digits no longer dim or grow as they arrive. In Clean the mail tile has a hairline like Clean's other tiles. The check keeps going while a mouse rests on the card; the person's own steps wait.
  • FormIts content sits 16px in from every edge (Clean's was 20px). Drafting and Creating use the moving highlight, Drafted gets the green tick, and Draft with agent and Create brief ease to the width of their words instead of jumping (Create brief no longer turns into a bare spinner). The Drafted marks sit at the end of their label's line, all on one right edge. Messages under the fields and the done screen ease the card to its new height instead of keeping the old one, and the done tick is the vivid green at its full size. A type chip's hover is a quiet fill, and nothing shrinks when pressed. The agent's draft keeps typing while a mouse rests on the card.
  • Payment formIts content sits 16px in from every edge (Clean's was 18 and 20px). The line under the card is now inside it, under Pay, and the summary's first row sits 16px under its line. On monthly billing the yearly discount folds away instead of fading to a struck-through grey. Processing uses the moving highlight with a spinner in the button's own colour (it was always white, which barely showed on Clean's white button in dark mode or on Blueprint's lime), and a paid button is a neutral button with the green tick in place of the lock, not a green fill. Nothing shrinks when pressed.
  • Number fieldIts content sits 16px in from every edge (Clean's was 17 to 20px). The summary under the fields is gone, as it only repeated their values; the line under the title now says a run stops at whichever comes first. In Clean the minus and plus are quiet grey circles in the text colour instead of blue squares, as Clean's controls are monochrome; Soft and Blueprint keep their accent. A stepper at its end keeps its shape with the disabled fill, so the steppers line up, and none of them shrinks when pressed.
  • CarouselNothing is faded any more: the slide peeking in at the edge shows in its full colours instead of blurred and washed out, and keeping a variant no longer dims the others. Each slide's caption sits 16px in from the slide's edges. Making 4 variants and each draft's progress use the moving highlight while the agent works. Keep eases to the width of Kept; in Clean, Kept stays the white button with a stronger edge and the green tick. The arrows, Keep and Retry show a quiet fill under the pointer, and the dots and the empty frame's picture mark are dark enough to see. The drafts keep building while a mouse rests on the card; the person's own steps wait.
