Motion pass: five components

The everyday motion of five components, refined: how popovers open from their trigger and go back into it, how rows, content and icons arrive, how a press and a hover answer, and how sizes change. Each frame is the component's own library page; use it, or press Replay. Every style keeps its character: Soft is plush, Blueprint crisp and quick, Clean calm. The two switches are ideas that would change a rule you set, so they are off until you turn them on.
