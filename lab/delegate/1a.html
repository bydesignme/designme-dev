Hand off to agents · 1A, hand-off board

Tasks wait in a tray; each agent is a column that fills with the hour it has booked. Pull a card into a column and, before you let go, see what it would take and cost; on the drop the finish time and the spend tick to their new values.

Mouse: drag a card into a column, between cards, or back to the tray. Keyboard: Tab to a card, Space to lift it, arrows to choose the agent and the place, Space to drop, Escape to cancel, Delete to send it back. Assign for me gives each waiting card to whoever would finish it first.
