Hand-off lab: six directions

Six takes on one idea: you hand tasks to a team of AI agents and keep control. Each plays a short loop and really works: drag tasks, switch the automatic option, pause agents. Each page has a Blueprint, Soft and Clean switch and light and dark. Pick the one (or the parts) to build into the library. This is a preview only, not on the site.

Boards and time

1A · Hand-off boardWaiting tasks are cards you pull into an agent's column; each column fills like a glass with the work booked, and its free-at time and spend count up as you drop.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
1B · The next hourEvery agent's queue on one timeline; a finish line shows when everything is done and moves as you assign. "Even out" flies work between lanes and pulls the line in.Clean · Blueprint · Dark

Routing and capacity

2A · SwitchboardEach task is scored against every agent; the winning route lights up in that agent's colour with a one-line reason, and the task travels down it. Turn routing off and pick yourself.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
2B · Load boardEach agent is a stack of its work measured against a one-hour line and a budget; when one runs over, drag work across or even it out and watch the stacks level.Clean · Blueprint · Dark

Command and canvas

3A · DispatchType a task; it proposes who should take it, when it would be done and what it would cost, shown on the team's live schedule before you approve.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
3B · Team canvasThe agents as a small map; drop a task on one, and when it's done the line to the next agent lights up and the task rides along it.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
