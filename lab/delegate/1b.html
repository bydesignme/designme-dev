Hand off to agents · 1B, the next hour

Every agent's queue on one shared hour, so you see who frees up when. Carry a waiting task onto a lane and it slots in where you point: what follows slides along, and the lane's free time, the spend and the line where everything is done move before you let go.

Mouse: carry a chip onto a lane, or a queued block to another place or lane, or back up to Waiting. Keyboard: Tab to a chip or block, Space to lift it, up and down for the lane, left and right for the place, Space to drop, Escape to cancel, Delete to send it back. Even out moves queued work to whoever finishes it first; Assign for me does the same for each task that waits.
