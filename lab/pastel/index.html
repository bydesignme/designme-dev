Pastel explorationNow vs a pastel variation of Soft, after the Focus app's colours

The recipe. One muted, slightly greyed hue per item (no orange except where something needs attention). Fills are that hue mixed only 16 to 18% into the surface, with a soft diagonal fade; text is the same hue mixed 62% into a blue-grey ink (#1b2230), so labels read tinted instead of saturated; marks and bars use the hue itself. Neutrals turn cool blue-grey, and tooltips become white cards with a hairline and a soft shadow instead of black. Every text pair passes 4.5:1 and APCA Lc 60 (light Lc 68 to 73, dark 62 to 70), every mark 3:1 or more. Soft only; Blueprint would keep its own colours.
