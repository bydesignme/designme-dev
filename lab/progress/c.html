Progress and meter, C: one run, one budget, in layers

The run and its cost read as one story. One bar carries the work and its five steps (point at a step for its time and credits), and the month's meter layers this run and what it should still take over what was used before, so it warns only when the run would cross 75% or 90%. The queue and the month's usage wait behind it as cards you can bring to the front.
