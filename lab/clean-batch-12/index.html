Clean, batch 12

Funnel chart, Top pages, Health score, Score breakdown and Eval radar with their Clean touches and your batch 1 and batch 2 notes, in all three styles. Switch the style and light or dark in the header; Animate plays a demo again, and Health score's accounts switch under its demo. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Funnel chartIn Clean the agent's note sits in an off-white strip along the card's foot, and everything else is 16px in from the card's edges. The bars still go from the accent to violet, now drawn from the fill blue, so the white counts on them read clearly in dark mode too. A count that won't fit on its bar (on a phone) sits just past the bar's end instead of spilling off it, and the card eases to a new height when a segment's note is a line longer or shorter.
  • Top pagesThe page names start where the title does: the rank numbers (which were in a code font) are gone from view, the rows are in rank order and screen readers still hear each rank. The views are right-aligned and the change chips end on the card's right edge; a row's hover fill reaches a little past its words. Bars stay one accent on a grey track, and the card is 16px in on every side.
  • Health scoreThe rows share the head's columns: each status dot sits under the ring and each name starts where the verdict does, as do an open row's details and the agent's line. In Clean a factor row's hover is a quiet grey fill in place of its hairlines. The agent's line eases to a new height when it changes, and on a phone a Weakest label goes under its name instead of cutting it short.
  • Score breakdownThe rows' bars are one accent colour on a grey track (no more rainbow); each row keeps its small key in its gauge segment's colour. The keys sit on the content's edge with the hover fill reaching past them, the first row is 16px under the line, and the card is 16px in on every side. Scoring shimmers while the gauge draws, the open row eases to each new height as the agent looks, proposes and applies, and 11 → 17 no longer breaks over two lines.
  • Eval radarIn Clean the line under the chart sits in an off-white strip along the card's foot. The average sits on an opaque chip, the legend's keys are drawn exactly as their series, its words end 16px in from the card's edge, and in Soft and Clean the rings are one quiet grey. While the chart draws, the line says Drawing the baseline with the moving highlight, and on a phone the spark stays on the first line of a long note.
