Clean charts

The Line chart and the Donut chart after the line chart and gauge you sent, with your 21 Nov notes on top: straight segments with sharp corners, a saturated line with a fill that fades from the top, and the comparison as a dotted line. They take the same anatomy in Clean, Soft and Blueprint, each in its own tokens. The Sales CRM's Forecast takes the new chart in one card, and its Reports show Team quotas as the new gauge. This is a preview only: the Sales CRM and the Dashboard builder below are copies inside this preview, and staging's own pages don't have these charts yet.

  • Line chartA quiet head (the title, a project picker with even room round its tile and arrow, the Day, Week and Month switch) over a hairline; the values on the left and dashed vertical gridlines. Today is a solid saturated line with a fading fill up to Now, marked with an open ring, and then carries on to the end of the day, week or month as a dotted projection over a fainter fill, so the chart always runs the full width. Yesterday is dotted in the palette's violet. The legend under the plot turns each line on and off. Pointing at the plot or the arrow keys on it show a dashed guide, a ring on each line, a white readout card (saying "Projected" past Now) and the hour as a grey chip on the axis. Blueprint's marks are square.
  • Donut chartNow a gauge: the week's agent hours as a half ring of parts in the chart palette, with small gaps and slightly rounded corners (square in Blueprint), the rest of the 40-hour budget as a light grey track, the total in the middle and the legend under it with a bar in each part's colour. Hovering a part or focusing a row lights both and puts that part in the middle.
  • Sales CRMForecast: the quarter's figures and the closed-won chart in one card, the chart across the full width, without Closing next. Reports: Stage conversion and Quota attainment are gone, and Team quotas sits beside Team activity as the new gauge, each rep's closed won a part of the team's quota; point at a rep to see them in the middle.
  • Dashboard builderIts Revenue and Visitors line charts and its Sales by channel donut take the same charts (Sales is in the sidebar).
