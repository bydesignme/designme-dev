Calm entrances, plush controls. Entrances grow out of a seed, slide out from behind the element before them and arrive as empty shapes that fill; numbers count while their measures draw; blur is the glue. They decelerate with no overshoot (560 to 600ms moves, 260ms fades, 70 to 90ms between items). Knobs, menus and presses are plush: knobs stretch toward where they go, menus grow out of their button like liquid, and presses spring back, with a gentle overshoot. Left is Soft now; right is Soft refreshed. Both play the same clicks; click inside a component and its entrance jumps to the end.
