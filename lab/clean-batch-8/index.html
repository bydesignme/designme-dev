Clean, batch 8

Data picker, Run drawer, Command palette, Memory panel and Agent settings in Clean, each on its own page, with your batch 1 and batch 2 notes carried through all three styles. Switch the style and light or dark in the header. Agent settings has its designs switch under the demo, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Data pickerIt sits centred in its stage with its list open: it used to sit about 47px high once a search shortened the list. The picker is on one set of columns (the steps' tiles and the search's glass in one column, the field names under the step names, the samples ending on the right edge), and a field's + stays on its first line when the tokens wrap. In Clean the steps' tiles are grey, the tokens are pills and the picker's note is a foot strip.
  • Run drawerThe step it waits on is a warning notice: a faint tint, the filled warning sign, the words in the text colour, Reject in white and Approve in the warning tint. Its content sits 16px in, rows' fills reaching out round it, and a running or picked step is no taller than the others. In Clean the tiles and bars are grey, keeping colour for the running step, the failed try and the retry.
  • Command paletteThe list is as tall as what it shows and the palette eases to its new height as you type, instead of keeping room. What ran sits on the footer's left with the keys on the right, its content 16px in, and a command no longer shrinks when it runs. In Clean the tiles are grey, Ask the agent has the ink tile and the footer is a foot strip.
  • Memory panelNo room is kept for the memory it learns: the panel grows as it arrives. The head's mark and each memory's mark share one column, the words another. No blue band behind a new memory (its New tag says it), a used memory pulses grey, and the head no longer repeats the count and the sources the list shows. It keeps playing under the pointer, as an agent at work.
  • Agent settingsContent 16px in, Save ending where the switches end, in all four designs. Live preview and Tabs keep no room for the bar or the tallest tab; the preview's facts are a plain list. Save keeps its width with a spinner, then the bar says Saved with the green tick. In Clean the switchers have a white knob, the sliders are ink, the bars are foot strips and the presets' tiles are grey. The presets' key shows only when the cells drop their words.
