V selects, H pans, S adds a sticky note, P draws, E erases, R draws a shape, A connects two items, T adds text and C adds a comment. Tab steps through what is on the board; Enter or a double-click writes on a note. The arrow keys move what is selected, or the view, Delete removes it and Shift F10 opens its menu. Plus and minus zoom, Shift 1 fits the board and Command Z undoes.
