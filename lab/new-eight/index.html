The eight coming back

The components taken out on 16 Nov, each with its Clean design and its Soft and Blueprint looks brought up to today's rules. Batch B is ready; batch A (Hand-off to a person, Steering, Browser session, Tool call) follows. Each name opens that component's page in Clean (?style=clean): change it to ?style=soft or ?style=bp for another style, and add &theme=light or &theme=dark for a mode. "Every style" means Soft, Blueprint and Clean; where a line names one style, only that style changed. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

What this is

  • These components were taken out of the library on 16 Nov and are coming back one by one as "New component live on DesignMe Dev" posts (owner, 23 Nov). Each now has a Clean design, and its Soft and Blueprint looks follow the rules decided since it left.
  • Each name opens the component's page in Clean. Nothing here is on staging's library yet.

Batch B

Voice orb

  • Clean: off-white head and foot strips that keep the card's round corners while it grows. Mute and End are the flat secondary; the primary is the ink button. In dark mode the orb follows Clean's white accent.
  • In every style, End is the white outlined button rather than a solid red fill, and Mute is the same outlined button with its word ("Mute", then "Unmute") and a slash across the microphone while muted.
  • The muted notice is the standard warning notice: a faint amber tint, the filled warning sign, the words in the text colour and Unmute as the warning action.
  • The footer sits under a hairline with 16px above and below. The card no longer keeps 88px for words that haven't come yet: it grows with what the agent says and stays centred.
  • Once the chat ends, the footer says so with the green tick, "Voice chat ended · Transcript saved", and Start again.
  • The live dot pulses in size and opacity instead of a coloured ring.
  • What is said fills its lines: each line of the script is a few words longer, and the caption is balanced, so no line ends on a single word at any width (owner, 24 Nov).

Image generation

  • Clean: off-white head strips on both panels, and head and foot strips in the larger view, whose head is one line. Style chips, settings, presets and ratings use Clean's one hover and selected fill. New pictures and the larger view appear without blur.
  • In every style, "Generating 4 pictures" has the loader's highlight sweep, and the progress and 2× chips over the pictures are solid so nothing shows through them.
  • Both panel heads have 16px above and below, and the composer, side panel, gallery and larger view sit 16px in, on phones too.
  • The larger view's actions sit under a hairline. While it is open, the panels under it are hidden so the dialog can't be read through.
  • The Styles panel lists its presets one to a row, a small picture beside the name and its line, so nothing is crammed (owner, 24 Nov). Where the panel is wide they sit two to a row; on phones, one.

Code viewer

  • Clean: an off-white head strip with the file switch 16px from its top and bottom.
  • In every style, the file names are no longer in a code font: the files are the library's segmented switch, as in Image generation's Gallery and Styles.
  • The head has 16px above and below, and the code is 16px from every edge. Line numbers, the note's mark and the note's words line up with the code.
  • The card no longer keeps a fixed height: switching files eases it to the new file's height.

Design tuner

  • Clean: head and foot strips on the inspector, Clean's slider track and knob, and the preview stage in Clean's card corners.
  • In every style, Tune again sits on the right in a footer under a hairline, instead of being a full-width button.
  • The agent's note is a plain line with a spark instead of a tinted box, and it folds open so the panel eases to its new height. "Agent is tuning" has the loader's highlight sweep.
  • The gaps between sliders are 12px, on the 4px scale.
