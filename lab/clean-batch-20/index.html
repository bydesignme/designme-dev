Clean, batch 20

The last five components in Clean, with your batch 1 and batch 2 notes and today's notes in every style. Switch the style and light or dark in the header, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • KanbanColumns and cards both have their content 16px from each edge. People sit side by side, in the header and on cards, and a picked face has a grey ring without fading the others. A card under the pointer, or picked, takes a quieter, deeper fill with no lift or shadow. The board is as tall as its tallest column and eases as cards move, and it keeps its height once you filter, fold or switch, so nothing moves under the pointer. The agent keeps working while you point at the board, but its card only moves to another column once the pointer leaves; its step shimmers like a loader. The header no longer repeats the counts (no "4 of 9 done" or "9 cards"). No cards match uses Clean's empty state. Labels keep their colours, a step lighter on dark cards so they stay readable. The Sales CRM board looks exactly as before.
  • Pricing cardsEvery plan has its content 16px from each edge, and the badge sits on the plan name's line in the accent's tint. A plan has no hover of its own. The editors slider is drawn like the library's Slider, and its count ends on the plans' right edge. Only the recommended plan's button is primary. On phones the billing switch and the slider stack.
  • StepperIn Clean the track is monochrome: the current step in an ink ring, done steps a white circle with the green tick, and the finish a neutral circle around the green tick. In every style the content sits 16px in, the current step has no tinted halo, a picked option has no shadow, and there is no empty room between the options and the buttons.
  • Set limitWhen the forecast passes the cap, the line under the bar becomes a warning notice like Model picker's: a faint tint, the filled warning sign and the words in the text colour. On track is a neutral line with the green tick. The amount bubble over the knob is gone (the figure in the head already shows it), the knob's hover ring is grey, the legend's forecast swatch has the bar's stripes, and the card grows with the notice.
  • Currency converterThe amount and its currency button now sit on one line, on one baseline. The currency lists open over the converter, down or up, without moving it. In Clean the currency buttons are flat pill triggers, and the content sits 16px in.
