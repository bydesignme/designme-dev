Clean, batch 14

Date picker, Segmented control, Date range picker, Event calendar and Agenda with their Clean touches and your batch 1 and batch 2 notes, in all three styles. Switch the style and light or dark in the header; Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Date pickerThe calendar's content is 16px in from its edges, with the month's arrows over the first and last columns of days; in Clean the calendar takes the card's 22px corners. While it is open the field's edge is one step stronger and its chevron turns over. A picked day no longer grows as it is clicked. The demo now types "next friday" and then closes on the day it found, so it ends on the closed field, centred, showing the date. It also opens again if another card's demo closed it while it was typing.
  • Segmented controlIn Clean the switch is Clean's: a white knob gliding on a grey track. The panel is as tall as the view on show and eases to the next one's height (a day with one event no longer keeps room for the month), and the card stays centred. The day's events are a plain list (they aren't buttons, so no grey card behind each), on the week's columns. Today has no tinted band behind its row: its date is an accent circle, as in Event calendar, and the month's today is the accent too. The last arrow sits on the content's right edge, and the card is 16px in on every side.
  • Date range pickerNow a dropdown, as range pickers are in most products: a field as wide as the other single fields that opens the ranges, From and To and two months under it, centred, and Apply closes it with the range in the field ("Sep 18 – Oct 19, 2026"). The field sits centred and glides up as the picker opens, like Date picker; on a phone the picker is as wide as the field, with the ranges as a row of chips over one month. How many days the range covers sits in the foot beside Clear and Apply, which in Clean is an off-white strip with 28px pills 8px from its edges.
  • Event calendarYour notes on the CRM's calendar (20 Nov) are here too: every event keeps its colours at full strength; an event's hover is a quiet, deeper fill in its own colour (no lift, no shadow, no grey ring), and the one whose card is open is a step deeper with a faint ring in its colour; in an event's card the time, people, notes and buttons start at the colour dot's edge, so both sides have the same room (16px all round, in Clean too). In Clean the week and the agent's button sit in an off-white head strip (the button a 28px pill 8px from the card's edges) and the days are 16px in from the card's sides; events stay ordinary rounded rectangles; Book is the ink button. Checking 4 calendars and Writing shimmer while the agent works, and the agent keeps working under a resting pointer. The card's close button is on the title's line with its glyph 16px in, the people sit side by side rather than stacked, and Prep notes stays with a green tick once the notes are in instead of disappearing. On a narrow calendar the head stacks rather than wrapping. The Sales CRM's calendar is unchanged.
  • AgendaThe head no longer repeats the count ("4 events"): the list shows them. Times start where Today does and titles, people and prep notes share one column. Now is a grey chip in the times' column with the accent line running from the bars to the right edge; its dot breathes in opacity, not a blue glow. The people sit side by side, each avatar with its own readable letters, and the card eases to its new height as notes open. 16px in on every side.
