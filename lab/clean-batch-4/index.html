Clean, batch 4

Five more components in Clean: Web search, Toast stack, Toast, Thinking states and Plan review. Each link opens its component page in Clean; the header switches style and light or dark, and Animate plays the demo again. Everything sits on shared columns: a row's mark is centred under the header's tile, its words start where the title's do, and its hover fill spans the content and no wider. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these yet.

  • Web searchA card of its own with the search head in a neutral tile; queries, the asked-again note and pages on the tile's column; grows and shrinks as the trail folds instead of keeping its tallest height.
  • Toast stackEach toast is a small card (16px in, 16px corners); only a passed check (green) and a warning (orange) take colour, on the icon alone; no blur; the deck is as tall as the toasts in it.
  • ToastA small card, not a pill; success green and error red on the icon alone, info and loading grey; the close button as tall as the button beside it; no blur, and the old toast fades before the new one rises.
  • Thinking statesStill bare, as a line in a chat; the mark's tile is a neutral rounded square with a hairline, the mark keeps the accent, and the seconds count in even figures.
  • Plan reviewThe header's mark in a 32px tile, each step's circle centred under it and its words under the title; ink spinner, orange wait ring, green done disc; an off-white foot strip with its buttons 8px from the card's edges.
