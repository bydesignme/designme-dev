Clean is a set of tokens, not new components. The same markup, copy and demos as the library, with clean.css laid over Soft: a light neutral stage, white cards with a hairline and one very soft, wide shadow, nested surfaces a hair off-white, squircle corners, lighter big figures, thin icons in grey tiles, the one blue accent with faint blue fades (area fills, a header wash, a fine hatch inside accent bars) and slower, calmer motion. Every text pair passes 4.5:1 and APCA Lc 60, every mark 3:1, in both themes.
