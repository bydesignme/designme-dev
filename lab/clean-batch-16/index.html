Clean, batch 16

Search input, Country select, Multi-select, Social sign-in and Sign in, each on its own page in Clean, with the batch 1 and 2 notes met in every style. Switch the style and light or dark in the header, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Search inputIts suggestions are now a list that opens from the field, as the other dropdowns do: under it, or over it when there is no room, with the field gliding up so the two stay centred. The glass, the rows' icons, the agent's spark and the group names share one column and the words another; the sources end where the field's cross does. The field's edge steps up while it is in use. The heading over the results says Results instead of repeating the count, and the rows no longer trail behind while the preview types. In Clean the list has 22px corners that its rows follow, and the Figma filter is a tag with rounded corners, not a pill.
  • Country selectThe list opens from the field (under it, or over it when there is no room) and the field glides up so the two stay centred, also while typing narrows the list. The search is the list's first row, its glass on the flags' column and its words on the names'; the flags and names line up with the field's, and the currencies stand in one column. The hint no longer peeks out at the list's corner. Flags stay rounded rectangles in Clean, and the list has 22px corners.
  • Multi-selectAs wide as the other single fields. Its list opens from the box (under it, or over it when there is no room), and the box and list stay centred together. The checkboxes stand on the column where the box's words start, with a clear 1.5px edge; in Clean they are rounded squares, ink once ticked. In Clean the chips are tags with rounded corners, not pills. The key the preview presses shows at the end of the line being typed on, and the first row is no longer cut off when the list opens again.
  • Social sign-inIts content sits 16px in from every edge in every style. Each provider is a flat secondary button as tall as a field, its mark and its words on the same two columns in every button. Last used is a plain grey tag inside the end of its button instead of a badge over the button's edge; on a narrow phone the buttons say only the provider's name, so Figma keeps its whole name beside the tag. The buttons no longer shrink when pressed, and signing in eases the card to the welcome's height instead of keeping the taller one.
  • Sign inIts content sits 16px in from every edge, and the card eases to each step's height instead of keeping room. A wrong email is said in red words after an alert mark under the field, with no red fill and no blank line kept for it. Verifying code and Opening your workspace use the moving highlight. A confirmed code is the green tick and plain words, with no green fill behind the digits, and the done check is green. Copy code is a secondary button that turns into a green tick and Copied, easing to its new width. The email's mark is an ink tile in Clean, and its shadow is no longer cut off.
