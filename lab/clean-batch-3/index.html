Clean, batch 3

Five components with their Clean touches, each on its own page in Clean (the style switch on each page shows Soft and Blueprint). Every edge was measured through the whole demo: content 16px from each card edge, controls in head and foot strips 8px from the card's edges, one centre line per row, and tools/site/check-align.js reports no issues in all three styles.

  • ThinkingA card of its own like Reasoning trail's: steps on a grey hairline, an ink ring for the step in hand, green discs for done ones, the first step 16px under the header's hairline; the header says what the work came to as the last step finishes, without a clock; it grows with its steps in every style
  • Rich text editorOff-white toolbar and foot strips with their controls 8px from the card's edges and the words 16px in; a picked format is white with a grey ring; Ask AI is a flat secondary button; Accept is ink with its key on it; the menu's heading is a label on the content's left edge; in Soft and Blueprint everything sits 16px in all round
  • Response versionsNo room kept for a longer answer, in any style: it grows with its words and keeps the old version's height only while a new one streams in; it keeps writing under the pointer; a copy is the green tick in the icon's place; the bar's first icon starts where the answer does
  • Feedback sheetSheet content 16px in with an off-white foot strip, Cancel and Send 8px from the card's edges; Send eases to the spinner's width; a rated thumb fills in ink, a picked reason is an ink chip, and the thanks is one line with a green tick
  • Answer action barNo room kept for a longer answer or an empty status; thumbs fill in ink, read aloud plays in ink and greys the word it reads, picked reasons are ink; a copy is the green tick; the model and time stay on the bar's line (on a phone, on the line under it)
