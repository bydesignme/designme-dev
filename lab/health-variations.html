Account health: three variations

Three ways to show an account's health in the CRM's details sheet, in place of the gauge and three bars. Each shows the same three accounts: healthy, worth watching and at risk. Hover the factors, press the agent's button, then pick one.

A Verdict first

A status in words and colour, one sentence on why, the agent's next step, then four quiet factor lines.

B Factor tiles

A small score ring and the verdict over a 2 by 2 grid of factor tiles, each with its figure and a tiny trend.

C Trend and drivers

A 12-week health line you can scrub, then the reasons as plus and minus points that add up to the score.
