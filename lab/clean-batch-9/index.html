Clean, batch 9

The six components that had their first Clean touches on 17 Nov, brought up to all of your batch 1 and batch 2 notes in every style. Each opens on its own page in Clean; switch the style and light or dark in the header. Agent tree has its designs under the demo, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Agent treeThe orchestrator's row no longer repeats the run's time and cost from the head, and the step that waits for you has no tinted band behind it. Running steps' words use the same moving highlight as every loader. In Clean the content sits 16px in, rows' hovers are concentric with the card, and the cost split strip is 44px. Cost tree has no summary under it (it said the head's words again and threw an error), and Cost map is only as tall as the levels it shows, with its line under the key opening only when it has something to say.
  • Run cost16px in from every edge in every style. The breakdown rows are plain text with no hover. In Clean the foot strip holds the switch, its hover round it, and the button as a 28px pill 8px from the card's edges; nothing presses or clips, and the button eases to the width of its new label.
  • Agent run timelineThe head has a neutral tile in the steps' icon column, so the steps' names start where the title starts. When the run ends the line under the title says what it did instead of repeating the time and retries the totals show. The finished playhead line is the vivid green. 16px insets, and a 44px totals strip in Clean.
  • Context meterThe near-full warning is a warning notice like the Model picker's, with Compact in the warning tint as the action that resolves it; the result is a plain line with the green tick, and the line takes no room until it has something to say. The bar uses the accent, violet, teal and a neutral grey, the ring the accent's mark colour, and Compact is a secondary button that eases to its new width. The pill no longer shrinks when pressed.
  • Status pillDone is a plain line with the green tick and no elapsed time. The Bar is a small card with its line and track 16px in, growing a little round Review while it is there, with Review on the right edge in every style. On a phone neither is wider than the card's room.
  • Meeting cardThe people are initials avatars side by side, no longer over each other and no longer photos, in vivid colours with white letters; on a phone the card shows the first two and counts the rest, so everything stays inside the card. The time chips are opaque and the now line passes under them. Join and the copy button don't shrink when pressed, the copy tick is green, and Join eases to the width of its new label. In Clean the track has round corners instead of a pill, and the foot strip's buttons are 28px, 8px from the card's edges.
