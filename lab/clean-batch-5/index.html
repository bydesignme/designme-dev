Clean, batch 5

Step loader, Decision prompt, Proposal card, Rewrite toolbar and Inline text diff, each on its own page in Clean, with your batch 1 and batch 2 notes carried through every style. Switch the style and light or dark in the header, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Step loaderClean's pill is Status pill's 36px hairline pill, and the step list is a round card whose ticks and loaders sit under the pill's loader and whose words start where the pill's do (in every style). Ticks are the bright green. Pointing at the pill gives it a quiet fill instead of a bigger shadow. When the steps start again the pill says the first step at once, never "Ready for review" over an empty track. Its chevron shows on designme.dev again.
  • Decision promptNo empty room under the answers: the card grows and shrinks with each question and the summary. Each answer is a white row with a faint edge; the picked one's edge is a step stronger and its number filled (ink in Clean). Clean has a foot strip with Skip and Continue 8px from the edges, and once you answer the strip says the agent is starting, shimmering. Buttons don't shrink when pressed. The first question is drawn with the card, so the docs' small live previews show it in Clean too (they showed the card without its question).
  • Proposal cardClean has a neutral icon tile and a foot strip with Not now and Accept 8px from the edges. Content is 16px in, in every style. The other options' rows are as wide as the content, with their swatches and values on the change's columns. The confidence meter uses the bright green, and "Applied to 3 buttons" is a neutral line with a green tick. On a phone the new colour sits under the old one, so nothing runs out of the box.
  • Rewrite toolbarNo room is kept for the rewrite: the new words appear one at a time, the card grows with them and shrinks once one is kept, and the toolbar glides down with the last line. Once a sentence holds a rewrite its selection tint goes, so no empty tinted band runs ahead of the words. "Rewriting" shimmers. In Clean the file mark is grey, the toolbar opens without blur, and Accept is a white button on the black toolbar. Struck words have a bright red line and the Rewritten tick is green.
  • Inline text diffRemoved words are red on their red tint and added words green on their green tint (both 4.5:1), with the strike in the bright red. The edit the agent isn't sure of opens as a warning notice: a faint amber tint, the filled warning sign, black words, Reject in the warning colours and Accept white. No room is kept for "Saved to Pricing v4": the card shrinks as edits fold in and grows for it at the end. The toolbar opens upwards when there is no room below, and on a phone the buttons get their own row so the count is never cut short.
