Clean, batch 15

Buttons, Badge, Progress, Slider and Tooltip, the most basic pieces of the library, each on its own page in Clean. Switch the style and light or dark in the header, and press Animate to play a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • ButtonsOne button per style in the first row (primary, soft, outlined, the warning action Sign in again, and ghost), with Run agent moved to States. A click never scales a button; in Clean only the ink primary presses. Buttons are as wide as their words and ease to a new width (Publishing, Copied) instead of keeping room. A done button turns to the outlined face with the vivid green tick, never a green fill. The icon buttons are 32px like the pills, and flat in Clean. Hold to delete shows red words on the tint and white words on the fill as it fills, and its fill is darker in dark mode so the white words read.
  • BadgeEvery tone's label keeps 4.5:1 and Lc 60 on its tint at rest and under the pointer (several failed under the pointer in light mode). Clean's neutral badge is a see-through grey, so Draft and Idle show on the stage. A row's name sits on the line of its first badges when the set wraps.
  • Progress and meterThis page shows Classic, which the CLI installs: 16px insets in Clean, the danger and paused colours as marks, the pause glyph on the bars' right edge, and its words shimmer while the run starts or runs. The library card's three designs had the same pass: Compact's Steps chevron at the row's end (the site's own .cv class had padded it 16px), the state dot and the steps' icons on one column, actions centred on the name and state, finished steps with the green tick; Steps' credits line as an off-white foot strip in Clean, done nodes without a green fill, the log on the text column; Budget's Details and pause on the bar's right edge, the cards behind showing only their lips and, in Clean, lips tall enough for 16px insets.
  • SliderThe value bubble over the knob is gone: it covered the value in the header and the agent's mark. The value reads beside the name as you drag. The panel grows with the sample line instead of keeping room for two lines, the content sits 16px in, the track has Clean's inner shadow, and in Clean the knob glides and lifts calmly, without the hop, lean and bounce.
  • TooltipThe line of text under the toolbar is gone, and so is the empty room kept for the card. The avatars sit side by side instead of overlapping, and are named for screen readers. The person card follows its avatar, sits under it or over it when there is no room, opens on keyboard focus, and has 16px insets in Clean, where it no longer blurs. The agent's status shimmers. The shortcut chip in a tip shows on Clean's white tip.
