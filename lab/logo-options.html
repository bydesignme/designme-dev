How the wordmark is kept

  • The DESIGNME letters and the ® are the nine paths of the current logo, copied without a single change to their outlines. Options set only their fill, their stroke or their position.
  • A script compares those paths in every logo on this page and in every download with the current logo’s, character for character.
  • The .dev in each option is set in Inter, the site’s typeface, and converted to outlines, so the SVGs need no font.
  • The downloads are the Soft, light versions with fixed colours. On the site each logo takes its colours from the style and theme.
