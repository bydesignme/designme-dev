Clean, batch 13

Four App basics components in Clean, each on its own page. Switch the style and light or dark in the header, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Scatter chartThe demo no longer breaks: it used to try to press Ask the agent why before the button was there, at some widths, and threw an error. In Clean the line under the chart is an off-white strip with Ask the agent why 8px from its corner, and the content is 16px in. The card grows with that line instead of keeping room for the longest answer. The legend's marks and the quadrant names sit on the chart's edges, the axis ends on the content's edge, and the readout over a dot looks like the page's other tooltips.
  • Activity heatmapClean shows the whole day again on the library card: its wider padding had pushed it into the phone layout, with cells that grew with the card. On a phone the grid now scrolls in fixed 20px cells, so a wider phone card never gets bigger cells; on wider cards all 24 hours share the width (checked on the landing page up to 720px in every style). In Clean a cell under the pointer gets a quiet grey ring instead of growing, and the key's squares have the cells' corners.
  • Stat cardsThe cards no longer lift when you point at them, because they aren't buttons: pointing at a change chip says what it is compared with. Agent spend falling is now green (good news) and rising is red, with a real minus sign. Each card is 16px in on every side, phones included.
  • Account tableEvery column lines up with its heading, the first 16px in, and MRR and Draft email end on the right edge; Seats are right-aligned with their heading. The search's glass sits over the logos and its words over the names. Hovering a row shows a rounded quiet fill 8px past the words with the lines around it hidden, and the open row no longer has a grey band. The note's spark sits under the logos and its words under the names. Draft email says Drafting with the loader's sweep, then Draft ready with the green tick, easing between widths. A search with no match shows Clean's empty state with Show all accounts. Rows open from the keyboard too, and rows a filter brings back no longer replay their entrance.
