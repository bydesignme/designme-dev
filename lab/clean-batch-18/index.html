Clean, batch 18

Banner, Comments, Notifications, Invite to collaborate and Reactions in Clean, with your notes from every batch in all three styles. Switch the style and light or dark in the header, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • BannerThe fourth style is now a warning, as your warning notices are everywhere: a faint warning tint, the filled warning sign before Heads-up, every word in the text colour, the drawing in the warning's orange and Add to calendar in the warning tint. On the coloured banners the main button is white in light mode in every style, Clean included, with a hairline edge and no shadow, and a quiet deeper fill on hover; in dark mode it is the accent (Clean's white ink). The words sit 24px in on every side when the text is at its tallest.
  • CommentsContent 16px from every edge, the pin on the avatars' column and the title where the comments' words start, and Resolve's tick on the same right edge as Send. No reply count in the head. Resolving keeps the thread in full colour: the pin turns green, a green Resolved chip shows and the reply field folds away. The thread grows as replies arrive with the field gliding down under it, and the agent keeps writing under the pointer. Your Agree takes the accent's tint, Send is the ink button in Clean, and Applied to 3 frames is a neutral line with the green tick.
  • NotificationsTwo columns as in Agent inbox: a bell tile in the head with the items' icons and avatars under it and their words where Inbox starts; Mark all read and the unread dots end on the right edge. The agent's icons sit on their status tints (green, amber, blue) instead of washed-out mixes, a read item keeps its colours (only its dot goes), and the card grows as items arrive instead of keeping room for them. An empty filter shows a small list sketch with one line.
  • Invite to collaborate16px insets with both hairlines running edge to edge and 16px under each; the members' avatars on the content's left edge and their roles on the right edge where the field ends; the role select in the field's own type so its words share the address's line. In Clean, Copy link and Send sit in an off-white foot strip 8px from the edges, and an address that isn't valid keeps a white chip with red words. Copy link turns into the green tick and Link copied, easing to its new width; Send keeps its width while it spins; the card grows as chips wrap.
  • ReactionsYour reactions take the accent's tint; hovering a reaction is a quiet fill rather than a darker ring; nothing shrinks when pressed and the blue ring that pulsed round a new reaction is gone (the emoji's little jump stays, calmer in Clean). The picker's emoji take a quiet fill on hover instead of growing.
