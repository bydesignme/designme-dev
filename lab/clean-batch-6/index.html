Clean, batch 6

Draft review, Eval results, Terminal stream, Citation hover card and Source stack, each on its own page in Clean, with your batch 1 and batch 2 notes applied in every style. Switch the style and light or dark in the header. Eval results has its designs under the demo, and Animate plays a demo again. This is a preview only: staging and the live site don't have these changes yet.

  • Draft reviewThe missing-attachment warning is now a warning notice like Model picker's: a faint amber tint, the filled warning sign, the words in the text colour, Attach the summary in the warning tint and Send anyway in white. Sent, scheduled and discarded show as a neutral row with the green tick (no green or blue fill), on the head's columns, and the header's tag steps aside while that row shows. In Clean the buttons and the result sit in an off-white foot strip, 8px from the card's edges, with the content 16px in. The card eases to its new height as things arrive, and Send eases to its new width.
  • Eval resultsTicks, bars and failed signs use the bright mark colours. The case being rerun stays in Failed until it has its result, and an empty filter shows the Clean empty state with a Show all cases button. Running lines shimmer. The subtitle no longer repeats the case count, and Run vs run drops the line that repeated its filters' counts. All three designs grow and shrink with what they show (Run vs run and Score & judge used to keep empty room). In Clean, failed and regressed rows have no red or amber fill, and the marks are rings. Score & judge is 16px in, its judge's part sits on one column, and its buttons beside each other are the same height.
  • Terminal streamThe terminal grows with its output up to 420px instead of keeping that height from the start. Only the output is mono; the directory, times, notes and summary use the interface's type. Everything is 16px in and sits on one column: the marks under the header's icon, the commands, output and notes on the directory's words. Lines down the left are grey, blue only while running and red if failed (no green or violet). Running shimmers in a grey tag, and the summary is a neutral line without a clock. In Clean: an off-white head strip with Stop and Copy 8px from the edges, no lines down the left, and the summary as the foot strip.
  • Citation hover cardNo empty room is kept under the answer for the card, so the answer sits centred. The card opens above its citation when there isn't room below, so it is never cut off. Citations take the blue tint when pointed at (no solid blue fill). The highlight in the quote is blue text on the tint, and the quote's line is grey. Source marks are bright fills with white letters, grey in Clean. The card is 16px in, and in Clean it scales and fades from its citation.
  • Source stackSource marks sit side by side in the pill instead of overlapping, as bright fills with white (or dark) letters in Soft and Blueprint, not pale tints, and one neutral grey in Clean. Row hovers are a quiet fill you can see on the stage. Every source's title starts on the same column, and the filter chips are spaced a little wider.
