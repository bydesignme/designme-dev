Toasts · variation 3b

Hold to keep

A toast you can feel: its edge drains as its time runs out, a press and hold fills it back until it clicks into place, and a sideways drag gives before it lets go.

Try it: Generate a picture, delete one with its bin. Press and hold a toast to keep it (or use its pin), drag it sideways to throw it away, point at it or focus it to stop its clock, Escape to close the newest. Undo, Retry and Open really do what they say.
