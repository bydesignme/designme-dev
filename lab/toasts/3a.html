Live pill

One pill on the app's top edge carries a whole agent run, update by update, and opens into a card only when there is more to say.

Try it: press Research. Point at the pill (or focus it) to open it, press it to keep it open, Escape or the chevron to fold it, Escape again or a swipe up to send it away until the run needs you. Raise or Stop really change the run; Open answer puts the answer in the thread.
