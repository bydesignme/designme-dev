Toast lab: six directions

Six takes on a toast people want to share. Each one leads with a tiny live glyph that is the news, and problems turn into their fix in the same toast. Each page plays a short loop and really works: press the actions, hover to pause, swipe a toast away. Each has a Blueprint, Soft and Clean switch and light and dark. This is a preview only, not on the site.

Morph and stack

1A · BoomerangSend lifts off and becomes the toast; the glyph is the message's own trip, and the undo countdown burns back along its path. Undo flies the toast back into the button and the draft returns.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
1B · DeckFive agents' news, each with its own live glyph (sources found, a picture developing, a budget gauge, an export, a hand-off). They tuck into a deck that fans out on hover; a problem turns into its fix in place.Clean · Blueprint · Dark

Agent work in a toast

2A · Job toastA "make slides" job lives in one toast: a hand of slide cards is dealt out as they're drafted, one turns red, gets fixed, and the hand closes into a deck with a tick.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
2B · Preview toastsEach result carries a live miniature (a sentence rewriting itself, a picture developing, a source being picked), and Insert or Cite flies it out of the toast into the page.Clean · Blueprint · Dark

Compact pill to rich card

3A · Live pillOne pill carries a whole agent run; its orbit glyph collects sources as dots, turns into a budget gauge, and the pill opens into a card only when there's something to decide.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
3B · Hold to keepA toast you can feel: a picture-frame glyph that develops, empties or fails; its edge drains as time runs out, hold it to pin it, throw it sideways to send it away.Clean · Blueprint · Dark
